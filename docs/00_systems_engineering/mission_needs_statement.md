# Mission Element Needs Statement (MENS)

## Mission

Provide an integrated financial intelligence environment that transforms authoritative public data into reproducible analytical evidence and decision-support information.

## Mission Needs

- MENS-001: Integrate authoritative economic and financial data.
- MENS-002: Preserve source provenance and timestamps.
- MENS-003: Validate data before analytical use.
- MENS-004: Support statistical and machine-learning analysis.
- MENS-005: Provide traceability from requirements to outputs.
- MENS-006: Support AI-assisted research without replacing authoritative evidence.
