# 12 Financial Intelligence Domains

1. **Economic Cycle** — GDP, inflation, employment, industrial production, housing, PMI, yield curve.
2. **Corporate Financial Health** — SEC filings, revenue, margins, cash flow, leverage, profitability.
3. **Banking System** — capital, liquidity, deposits, loans, NIM, bank health.
4. **Institutional Investors** — 13F, 13D/13G, Form 4, fund ownership and concentration.
5. **Credit Markets** — spreads, yields, high-yield/investment-grade stress and default indicators.
6. **Derivatives Markets** — futures, options, swaps, positioning and hedging signals.
7. **Structured Finance** — MBS, ABS, CMBS, RMBS, CLO and securitized-credit metrics.
8. **Consumer Economy** — spending, household debt, savings and consumer credit stress.
9. **Market Sentiment** — volatility, short interest, positioning and investor expectations.
10. **International Finance** — trade, current account, capital flows, FX and foreign Treasury holdings.
11. **Monetary Policy** — policy rate, balance sheet, reserves, liquidity, QT/QE and FOMC information.
12. **Risk & Compliance** — enforcement, regulatory actions, audit findings and consumer/regulatory risk.
