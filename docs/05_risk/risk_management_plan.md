# Risk Management Plan

## Categories
Technical, data, model, market, credit, liquidity, operational, cybersecurity, regulatory, compliance, privacy, bias, explainability, drift, cost, schedule, vendor, integration, governance.

## Process
Identify → Analyze → Prioritize → Mitigate → Monitor → Communicate → Close/Accept

## Model Risk
Document training data, assumptions, features, target, evaluation metrics, limitations, validation evidence, version, and deployment status.
